Beardworks Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-25 · Beardworks (털보네공업사, sole proprietorship, Republic of Korea) · Covers: beardworks.io and its subdomains, and the games Naïl: Under the Roots and Knellroot (all versions)

The only personal data we receive is a message you choose to send us. Our games and website have no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and game progress stays on your device. We do not sell or share personal data.

1. What we receive

WhenWhatWhyLegal basisHow long
You email us or use the contact formName, email address, topic, messageTo replyKorea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), Article 15(1)(4) (steps taken at your request) — no consent neededOne year after the inquiry is closed. We delete sooner on request.

We receive nothing else (no ID numbers, sensitive data, payment details or location). The contact form counts your IP address for one minute to stop repeated submissions and does not keep it. We process no data on the basis of consent and provide none to third parties (lawful requests from authorities excepted).

3. Games

  • Progress and settings are processed only on your device and are not sent to any server (browser storage for the web version, the app's data folder elsewhere). Erase a save slot, clear the site data in your browser, or uninstall the app to remove them. Source code: Naïl · Knellroot
  • The Steam version talks to the Steam client to unlock achievements and uses your account number only as the name of the save folder on your device. The Epic Games Store version signs in to your Epic account to unlock achievements and keeps the account identifier only in memory while running. Neither reaches us.
  • Store accounts, purchases and payments are handled by each store: Valve · Epic Games. We see only totals such as units sold.

4. Website and cookies

  • Pages and downloads are hosted by GitHub; the web versions of the games by Cloudflare and Netlify. Access records (IP address and the like) may remain on their servers; we do not receive or use them.
  • We set no cookies of our own. Cloudflare, in front of every page, sets a security cookie, cf_clearance, to tell bots from people (up to one year; details). You can block or clear cookies in your browser and everything keeps working.
  • Do Not Track: we do not track you across websites or let others do so here, so nothing changes in response to a Do Not Track signal.

5. Deletion and security

When the retention period ends or you ask, the privacy officer deletes the message from the mailbox without delay and empties the deleted items so it cannot be restored (records a law requires us to keep are kept separately for that period only). To keep data safe we ask only for what we need, use encrypted connections (HTTPS) only, store messages on no server, and keep them in one mailbox that only the owner can access. We keep no paper copies.

6. Your rights and contact

  • Wherever you live, you (or your legal guardian, if you are under 14) can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your personal data, or to have us stop using it. Email support@beardworks.io from the address you used; we act within 10 days.
  • Privacy officer: Myung-jin Cho (조명진), owner · support@beardworks.io
  • Our games and website are not directed to children, and we do not ask anyone's age. If we learn a child under 14 has written to us, we reply once and delete the message.
  • If you live outside Korea (including the EEA and the UK): Beardworks, in the Republic of Korea, is the controller of your data; we have no data protection officer and no representative in the EU or the UK. We process your message on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Sending a message is voluntary, but without it we cannot reply. Cloudflare and Microsoft are based in the United States; their policies state that they rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (with the UK Extension) and standard contractual clauses, and you can ask us for details. Besides the rights above, you can ask us to restrict processing, object to it, or receive your data in a portable form; we answer free of charge and within one month at the latest. You can complain to us, and to the data protection authority where you live (in the UK, the Information Commission). We make no automated decisions and do no profiling.
  • In Korea you can also contact the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee, 1833-6972 (kopico.go.kr); the Personal Information Infringement Report Center, 118 (privacy.kisa.or.kr); or the National Police Agency, 182 (ecrm.police.go.kr).
