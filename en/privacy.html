Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-24 · Operated by Beardworks (털보네공업사, sole proprietorship, Republic of Korea) · Covers: Naïl: Under the Roots and Knellroot (all versions, including development builds), beardworks.io and its subdomains

In short

Our games and this website do not collect personal data on their own. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking cookies, and your progress is saved only on the device you play on. The store versions (Steam and the Epic Games Store) connect to your store account only to unlock achievements; your store account itself is covered by that store's own privacy policy. The only personal data we receive is a message you choose to send us (by email or through the contact form).

1. What the game saves on your device

  • Progress (save slots, unlocks, journal and so on) and settings (sound, language, controls and so on). No name, email or location is included.
  • Where: the browser's site storage (localStorage) for the web version; the app's save folder on the device for PC, Android and iOS.
  • This data is never sent to us. The games have no code that sends anything to a server of ours (the source code is public: Naïl · Knellroot).
  • To delete it: erase a slot on the game's save slot screen, clear the site data in your browser, or uninstall the app.

2. Store versions

Steam version

  • The game talks to the Steam client to unlock achievements. If you turn on Steam Cloud, the Steam client syncs the game's save files.
  • The game reads your Steam account number only to name the save folder on your device (so several accounts on one PC keep separate saves). It does not send this number anywhere.
  • Your Steam account is covered by Valve's own policy: Valve Privacy Policy

Epic Games Store version

  • The game signs in to your Epic account with the login the Epic launcher hands over, and unlocks achievements through Epic Online Services.
  • For this the game receives an account identifier from the sign-in. It keeps the identifier in memory only while the game is running, does not save it, and does not send it to us. The game does not use your display name or any other profile data.
  • Your Epic account is covered by Epic's own policy: Epic Games Privacy Policy

Purchases and payments take place in each store. We only see aggregate statistics (units sold, achievement rates and so on) and never receive an individual buyer's payment details.

3. Website, web version and downloads

  • This website (beardworks.io, nail.beardworks.io and knellroot.beardworks.io), the downloads (APK and PC builds) and the source code are hosted by GitHub; the web versions of the games are hosted by Cloudflare and Netlify; DNS for the domain is handled by Cloudflare. The Naïl game page loads its latest news from GitHub through our Cloudflare Worker (api.beardworks.io).
  • When you visit, access logs such as your IP address and browser details may be kept in those companies' server logs. We do not receive or use those logs. Policies: GitHub · Netlify · Cloudflare
  • This website and the web version set no cookies of our own. Cloudflare, which sits in front of every page, sets a cf_clearance cookie as a security feature to tell automated traffic (bots) apart (up to one year; Cloudflare cookies). It is not used for advertising or analytics, and we do not receive its contents. The contact page also runs Cloudflare Turnstile in your browser to check for bots (see section 4). The web version stores (caches) the game files in your browser so it can start offline.

4. Emails and the contact form

  • If you email support@beardworks.io (questions) or business@beardworks.io (work inquiries), we use your address and message only to reply. Mail is forwarded through Cloudflare Email Routing to the operator's Outlook.com mailbox and kept there (Microsoft Privacy Statement).
  • If you use the contact form on our contact page, your topic, name, email and message pass through a Cloudflare Worker (api.beardworks.io) and arrive as a single email in the same Outlook.com mailbox. The form's contents are not stored on this website or in the Worker, and are used only to reply.
  • The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to block automated submissions. Turnstile processes only the browser and connection information (such as your IP address) needed to check for bots, and does not read what you write in the form (Cloudflare Turnstile privacy addendum). To prevent repeated submissions we briefly count requests per IP address, but we do not keep it in the email or any log.
  • Ask us to delete it once your question is answered and we will. We never use it for anything else or share it.
  • Anything you post on GitHub Issues is public on GitHub and covered by GitHub's policy.

5. Children

We do not ask for age and do not collect personal data from anyone, including children. If we learn that a child has contacted us, we delete the message after replying.

6. Your rights and contact

The only personal data we hold is messages you sent us (by email or the contact form), and you can ask to see or delete it. For data in your store account, contact that store. Privacy contact: the owner of Beardworks, support@beardworks.io

7. Changes

If our games or website ever start handling new data, we will update this page first and change the effective date. Earlier versions are in the change history.
