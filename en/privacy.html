Beardworks Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-25 · Operated by Beardworks (털보네공업사, sole proprietorship, Republic of Korea) · Covers: beardworks.io and its subdomains, and the games Naïl: Under the Roots and Knellroot (all versions, including development builds)

Beardworks (털보네공업사 — the website beardworks.io and the games Naïl: Under the Roots and Knellroot) publishes this privacy policy under Article 30 of Korea's Personal Information Protection Act to explain how we handle and protect personal data and to resolve related complaints quickly.

In short

  • What we receive — the name, email address and message you choose to send us by email or through the contact form. We use them only to reply.
  • What we do not receive — our games and website have no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Game progress stays on the device you play on and never reaches us.
  • What we pass on — we do not sell or share personal data. Messages travel through the overseas companies that forward and store our mail (Cloudflare and Microsoft; section 4).
  • To delete — email support@beardworks.io and we will delete your message.

2. What the games save on your device

  • Progress (save slots, unlocks, journal and the like) and settings (sound, language, controls). They contain no name, email or location.
  • Where: the web version uses the browser's site storage (localStorage); the PC, Android and iOS versions use the app's data folder on the device.
  • These files are processed only on the device and are not sent to any external server. The game code has no feature that sends anything to our servers (the source is public: Naïl · Knellroot).
  • To delete them: erase the slot on the game's save-slot screen, clear the site data in your browser, or uninstall the app.

3. Store versions

Steam

  • The game talks to the Steam client to unlock achievements. If Steam Cloud is on, the Steam client syncs the game's save files.
  • The game reads your Steam account number and uses it only as the name of the save folder on your device (to keep saves apart when several accounts share a PC). The number never leaves the device and disappears when the save folder is deleted.
  • Your Steam account is covered by Valve's policy: Valve Privacy Policy

Epic Games Store

  • The game signs in to your Epic account with the login passed by the Epic launcher and unlocks achievements through Epic Online Services.
  • To do so it receives an account identifier at sign-in. It is kept only in memory while the game runs; it is not stored or sent to us. We do not use your display name or other profile data.
  • Your Epic account is covered by Epic's policy: Epic Games Privacy Policy

Purchases, payments and downloads happen in each store, and the store handles that data. We see only totals such as units sold and achievement rates, never an individual buyer's details.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data, share it for advertising, or provide it to third parties. The only exception is a lawful request from an authority. If that ever changes, we will update this policy first and ask for consent where required.

6. Website logs, cookies and Do Not Track

  • This website (beardworks.io, nail.beardworks.io, knellroot.beardworks.io), the downloads (APK, PC builds) and the source code are hosted by GitHub; the web versions of the games are hosted by Cloudflare and Netlify. Cloudflare handles DNS for the domain. The Naïl page reads the latest news from GitHub through our Cloudflare Worker (api.beardworks.io).
  • When you connect, access records such as your IP address and browser details may remain in those companies' server logs. We do not receive or use those logs. Policies: GitHub · Netlify · Cloudflare
  • We set no cookies of our own. Cloudflare, which sits in front of every page, sets a cf_clearance cookie as a security measure to tell automated traffic (bots) from people (up to one year; Cloudflare cookies). It is not used for ads or analytics, and we do not receive its contents.
  • To refuse cookies, block or clear them in your browser settings. The pages and games keep working (you may see the bot check again).
  • On the contact page, Cloudflare Turnstile runs in your browser to check for bots. It processes only the browser and connection data needed for that check and does not read what you type in the form.
  • The web versions store the game files in your browser (cache) so they can start offline.
  • Do Not Track: we do not track you across websites and do not let other parties do so on this site, so nothing changes in response to a browser's Do Not Track signal.

7. Deletion

We delete personal data without delay once it is no longer needed. Procedure: the privacy officer (the owner) checks which messages have passed the retention period or have a deletion request, and deletes them. Method: we remove the message (and our reply) from the mailbox and empty the deleted items so it cannot be restored. We keep no paper copies. If a law requires us to keep a record longer (for example, records of a contract or payment), we keep only that record for that period and tell you so.

8. Security measures

  • Organisational — we ask only for what we need to reply, and only the owner handles personal data.
  • Technical — the contact form and website use encrypted connections (HTTPS) only. Messages are not stored on the website or on a server, only in one mailbox, and only the owner has access to it.
  • Physical — we keep no paper copies or separate storage media.

9. Children

Our games and website are not directed to children. We do not ask anyone's age and do not knowingly collect personal data from children (under 13 in the United States, under 14 in Korea). If we learn that a child has written to us, we reply once and then delete the message. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child's message at any time.

10. Your rights and choices

  • You (or your legal guardian, if you are under 14) can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your personal data, or to have us stop using it.
  • How: email support@beardworks.io from the address you used to contact us. We act and reply within 10 days. A representative needs to show that you authorised them.
  • The only personal data we hold is your message. For store account data, ask the store; for posts on GitHub, ask GitHub.
  • California and other US residents: we do not sell or share personal data and do not use it for targeted advertising. You can make the requests above the same way, and we will not treat you differently for doing so.
